What it is

  • Your timeline, in order. Newest last, nothing reordered, nothing injected.
  • Runs in your browser. There is no server in the middle — your account talks to your server, and your settings stay on this device.
  • Read before you commit. Browse anonymously as long as you like. Log in when, and if, you want to.
  • Bring more than one. Mastodon and Bluesky accounts side by side, plus RSS feeds and other sources in the same reader.
