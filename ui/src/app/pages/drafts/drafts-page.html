Nothing in progress. A draft can live in four places:

  • 💾 Local — saved in this browser. Private and instant, but stays here.
  • ⏳ Parked — a scheduled post dated far enough out that it never fires. Kept by the server, so it follows you.
  • 🔒 Self — a post to nobody but you. Follows you too, but your instance admin can read it.
  • 📋 Paste — text parked at a paste service. Reachable anywhere via its link, and not private.

While composing, hit 💾 to save one — anything half-written is auto-kept for you anyway.
