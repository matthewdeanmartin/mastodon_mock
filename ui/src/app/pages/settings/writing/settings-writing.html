✍ Writing

Tag a draft or a private post #NOTE or #TODO and it becomes a productivity object: it shows up beside the editor in Write, instead of you having to go looking for it.

A note is something you wrote down to keep. A to-do is something you owe a reply to — "answer this later", "write about this later". Both live either in this browser as drafts, or on the server as posts only you can see.
