Where writing starts

On Home
Off by default. Home shows a Write button, which opens the full editor, next to a Quick post button that expands the small box for that visit only. Turn this on to have the box already open every time, the way it used to be. Stronger than the setting above, and it wins over it: Home offers no Quick post at all, and the editor only saves. You post later, from Drafts. Replies, chats and paste shares are never held back — they're urgent, or already deliberate.

Notes and to-dos

Tag a draft or a private post #NOTE or #TODO and it becomes a productivity object: it shows up beside the editor in Write, instead of you having to go looking for it. A note is something you wrote down to keep. A to-do is something you owe a reply to. Both live either in this browser as drafts, or on the server as posts only you can see.
