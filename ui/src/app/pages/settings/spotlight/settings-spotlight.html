Endorsements

The ads in the right rail are house endorsements — our own projects, and things we think are worth your time. Nobody pays for them, no third party is involved, and nothing about you leaves this browser.

Only {{ shown }} are shown at a time, and they change every half hour. The donate links further down the rail are not ads and stay put.
