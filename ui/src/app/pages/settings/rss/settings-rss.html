📡 RSS feeds

Any RSS or Atom feed becomes posts in your home feed, read-only, with an "Open original" link. No account, no credential — a feed URL is all it takes.

Mockingbird has no server, so a feed only works if its site allows browser (CORS) access. Plenty don't. You'll know right away when you add one, because adding it fetches it.

Up to 10 feeds can be saved in this browser.
