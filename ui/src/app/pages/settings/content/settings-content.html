🙈 Content warnings & sensitive media

A content warning is the author's judgement about their audience, not about you. For people you read all the time, clicking through the same warning every day is friction without safety — so you can switch it off, either for everyone or for specific people.

This is the flipside of Muted & Blocked: that page is for accounts you want less of, this one is for accounts you want without a doorway in front of them.

Everyone

Applies to every post from every account, on this Mastodon account only. Stored in this browser — it is a reading preference, not something the server knows about.

Posts with a content warning render already open. The warning text is still shown above the post, so you always know one was set.

Images and video flagged sensitive render without the blur.

Note: Appearance has an "Always expand posts marked with content warnings" box. That one is a server-side preference saved to your Mastodon account for other apps to honour — this one is what Mockingbird itself obeys.
