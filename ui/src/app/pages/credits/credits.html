Paste & short-link services

When you publish a draft as a paste or short link, the text is sent directly from your browser to the third-party service you pick — we never see or store it. Your use of these services is governed by their own terms of service and privacy policies, not ours. Review them before posting anything you would not want a third party to host:

  • Pastepile — anonymous pastes with an optional public feed (terms of service).
  • Rentry — anonymous Markdown pages. Rentry publishes no dedicated terms page; see rentry.co/what for its usage notes.
  • TinyURL — a link shortener used to store a short message inside the link itself. See tinyurl.com for its terms of use. These links are permanent and public — anyone who has the link can read the message, and it cannot be edited or deleted afterwards.

Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter, a lightweight, open-source hit counter, to get a rough idea of how many people visit and which pages they land on. GoatCounter is designed to be privacy-friendly: it sets no cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not collect or store personal data. It records only aggregate, anonymous page-view counts — no advertising, no profiles, nothing sold to anyone. See goatcounter.com for exactly what it does and how it is implemented.

Everything else — your login, preferences, drafts, and local settings — stays in your own browser's storage. It is not sent to us; we don't run a server that could receive it. Requests for posts, timelines, and the like go straight to whichever Mastodon server you are using, under that server's own privacy policy.
